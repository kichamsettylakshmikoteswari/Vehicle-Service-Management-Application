# Vehicle-Service-Management-Application
Urban Fleet Operations offers vehicle servicing to individual customers across its service centres. Currently, customers raise service requests through email and phone calls, leading to delays, lack of visibility, and inefficient tracking of service history.

To eliminate these operational bottlenecks, Urban Fleet Operations is building a Vehicle Service Management application on Pega Platform™ to:

Allow customers to raise vehicle service requests.

Enable service advisors to inspect vehicles and provide estimates.

Capture customer approvals before service execution.

Assign technicians and track service completion.

Automatically notify customers upon service completion.
